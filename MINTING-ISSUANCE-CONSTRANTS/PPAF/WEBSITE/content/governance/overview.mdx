# Alliance Governance

The Passport Alliance operate as a multi-stakeholder governance body. Its primary mission is to define and maintain the **Agent Passport Issuance Standard (APIS)**.

## The Passport Alliance Foundation (PAF)

The PAF is a non-profit foundation responsible for the stewardship of the APIS standard. It is modeled after established governance bodies like ICANN and the Linux Foundation.

### Core Objectives
1.  **Maintain APIS**: Evolve the technical specification through the RFC process.
2.  **Accreditation**: Certify Tier 2 issuers who meet technical and security requirements.
3.  **Registry Operations**: Maintain the global directory of accredited issuers.
4.  **Community Outreach**: Promote the adoption of machine identity standards.

## Organizational Structure

### 1. Steering Committee
Responsible for high-level strategy, financial oversight, and final approval of major spec changes.

### 2. Technical Committee
Technical experts from member organizations who review RFCs and technical documentation.

### 3. Issuer Working Group
Focuses on the operational requirements for Passport Issuers and the federation protocol.

## The RFC Process

Standardization is driven by the community through the **Request for Comments (RFC)** process.

1.  **Draft**: A member submits a proposal.
2.  **Review**: 30-day public comment period.
3.  **Last Call**: 14-day final review for critical objections.
4.  **Adoption**: Formal vote by the Technical Committee.

## Membership Tiers

- **Founding Members**: Organizations that contribute core infrastructure and oversight.
- **Certified Issuers**: Organizations that run accredited Tier 2 implementations.
- **General Members**: Technology partners and developers supporting the standard.
