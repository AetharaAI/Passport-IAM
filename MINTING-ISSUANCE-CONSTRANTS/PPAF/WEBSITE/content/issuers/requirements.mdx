# Accreditation Requirements

To maintain the integrity of the Passport Alliance, all issuers must meet specific technical and governance criteria. We define two tiers of compliance.

## Tier 1: Self-Certified Issuer

Tier 1 issuers can mint passports and mandates within their local trust realms. They are suitable for internal deployments and private ecosystems.

### Technical Checklist
- [ ] **Discovery**: Host a `/.well-known/passport-issuer` document.
- [ ] **JWKS**: Publish public keys via a standard JWKS endpoint.
- [ ] **Signatures**: Support Ed25519 signing for all issued credentials.
- [ ] **DIDs**: Issue passports using the `did:passport:` method.
- [ ] **Compliance**: Pass the [Passport Compliance CLI](https://github.com/passport-alliance/compliance-tool) Tier 1 suite.

---

## Tier 2: Certified Issuer (Alliance Accredited)

Tier 2 issuers are the "Gold Standard" of the Alliance. They participate in the global federation, allowing their agents to be recognized across the entire network.

### Additional Requirements
- [ ] **NATS Bridge**: Run a NATS Federation Bridge for real-time revocation propagation.
- [ ] **Revocation**: Propagation latency must be under 30 seconds globally.
- [ ] **Audits**: Undergo an annual security review and technical audit.
- [ ] **Policy**: Publish a public Issuance and Revocation Policy.
- [ ] **Uptime**: Maintain 99.9% availability for discovery and status endpoints.

## The Application Process

1.  **Run the Tests**: Download and run the Compliance CLI against your instance.
2.  **Submit Results**: Upload your test report via the [Application Form](/issuers/apply).
3.  **Review**: Alliance members will review your submission and policy documents.
4.  **Accreditation**: Once approved, your issuer DID will be added to the Alliance Registry.
