## How It Works (The Simple Version)

Think of Agent Passports like **a driver's license for AI**, but with three layers of security:

### Layer 1: The Birth Certificate (Issuer)

Just like a government issues birth certificates, an **Issuer** (like your company's IT department) creates the AI's identity:

- The AI gets a unique ID number (like a Social Security Number)
- This ID is cryptographically signed (like a hologram on your passport)
- Anyone can verify it's real (like scanning a QR code)

**Analogy:** *When you're born, the hospital issues a birth certificate with a stamp. That stamp proves the government verified your identity.*

---

### Layer 2: The Power of Attorney (Principal)

Just like you might give someone power of attorney to act on your behalf, a **Principal** (a person or company) authorizes the AI:

- The Principal creates a **Mandate** (permission slip) saying what the AI can do
- Example: "This AI can read emails, draft responses, but NOT send without approval"
- The Mandate is cryptographically signed (like a notarized document)

**Analogy:** *If you're traveling and want your spouse to sign documents for you, you give them a notarized power of attorney. It says exactly what they can and can't do.*

---

### Layer 3: The Action Log (Agent)

Every time the AI does something, it's like writing in an official logbook:

- The AI digitally signs every action (like signing a form)
- The signature proves: "This specific AI performed this specific action at this specific time"
- No one can fake or change the log (it's cryptographic proof)

**Analogy:** *When you swipe your key card at work, it logs who entered which room at what time. But with AI, the log is mathematically impossible to forge.*

## The Three-Party Chain (Explained Simply)

Imagine you're buying a house. For the sale to be legal, you need:

1. **Government** verifies the property exists (public records)
2. **Seller** signs the deed over to you (notarized)
3. **You** sign the mortgage (witnessed by a notary)

If any link in this chain is missing or forged, the sale isn't legal.

**Agent Passports work the same way:**

1. **Issuer** verifies the AI exists and is legitimate
2. **Principal** signs authorization for the AI to act on their behalf
3. **Agent** signs every action it takes

**If something goes wrong,** you can trace it back:
- Was the action signed by the AI? (Yes/No)
- Did the Principal authorize this action? (Check the Mandate)
- Is the Issuer trustworthy? (Check their credentials)

**Result:** Bulletproof accountability. No one can deny what happened.
